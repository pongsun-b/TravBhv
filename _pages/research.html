Research

How movement becomes evidence

TBRG sits in the Transportation Engineering division of the Department of Civil Engineering at Chulalongkorn University. Work runs from travel decisions outward: surveys and models of how people move, then engineering and planning that can act on that evidence.

Lines of work

  • Transportation engineering Safe, efficient movement of people and goods, and the systems that carry that movement.
  • Travel behavior surveys and analysis Mode, time, purpose, and the preferences behind those choices.
  • Transportation planning What the network needs next, from current use to future demand.
  • First-mile walk access Network isochrones from station exits on the OpenStreetMap walk graph — 5, 10 and 15 minutes at a typical 4.0 km/h, with river and canal boat piers as an extra layer. Access map.

A related lab project, the Public Transport Accessibility Index (PTAI), measures demand-weighted generalized travel cost across 88 inner-Bangkok subdistricts. The Access map is the walk to rail, not that index.
