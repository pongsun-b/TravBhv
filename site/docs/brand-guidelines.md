# TBRG brand guidelines

The written half of the identity. The visual half is the `/brand/` page on the site, which renders
the same tokens and also prints the measured contrast ratios.

- Source of truth for values: `src/lib/tokens.css`
- Machine-readable mirror: `design-tokens.json` (generated by `npm run tokens`)
- Logo artwork: `brand/svg/` (master), `brand/png/` (raster), `brand/favicon/` (icons)

---

## 1. What TBRG is

TBRG is the Travel Behavior Research Group, in the Department of Civil Engineering at Chulalongkorn
University. The name is the group's own, restored from its previous site; TBRG is the short form of
Travel Behavior Research Group. The identity has to do one job: make a research group that studies
movement look precise, current and calm, without looking loud.

**Positioning in one line.** We study the decisions inside a journey, and we publish what can be reused.

## 2. Voice

- Plain, specific, unhurried. Prefer the concrete noun to the abstract one.
- Numbers with a source. If a figure is illustrative, say so next to it.
- No marketing intensifiers: no "seamless", "next-generation", "revolutionary", "empowering".
- Sentence case for headings. No title-case shouting.
- Do not use em dashes anywhere in product copy. Use a comma, a colon or a full stop.

## 3. Logo

Two marks, one system.

| Element | File | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mark | `tbrg-mark.svg` | The node-on-ring symbol. Default, 24 px and above. |
| Mark, small cut | `tbrg-mark-small.svg` | Heavier stroke and wider opening for 24 to 32 px. |
| Mono mark | `tbrg-mark-mono.svg` | Single-colour reproduction, `currentColor`. |
| Monogram | `tbrg-monogram.svg` | The letter T. Secondary mark. |
| Monogram tile | `tbrg-tile.svg` | Two-colour tile for app icons and favicons. |
| Wordmark | `tbrg-wordmark.svg` | Outlined TBRG lettering, no font required. |
| Primary lockup | `tbrg-lockup-horizontal.svg` | Mark then wordmark. Default. |
| Stacked lockup | `tbrg-lockup-stacked.svg` | Mark above wordmark above descriptor. |

The mark is a **node on a ring**: a closed route with one stop, with the ring opened just enough
that the node keeps clear air on both sides. That opening is the whole point. A closed ring with a
dot reads as a target; a ring with two gaps reads as a loading spinner. One opening, one node.

The wordmark is Instrument Sans SemiBold, outlined, tracking `+0.045em` with pair kerning on A-L,
L-I and I-S so the gaps read evenly.

### Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the **node diameter** (about one mark-stroke width, roughly 12% of the
mark's height) on all four sides. Nothing may enter that zone.

### Minimum sizes

| Element | Digital | Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary lockup | 160 px wide | 32 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 96 px wide | 22 mm wide |
| Mark (display cut) | 32 px | 8 mm |
| Mark (small cut) | 24 px | 6 mm |
| Monogram tile | 16 px | 5 mm |

Below 16 px, use the monogram tile alone. No abstract mark survives a 16 px anti-aliased render.

### Correct and incorrect

Correct:

- Use the supplied SVG files. They are the master artwork.
- Keep the clear space clear.
- Use the monogram tile below 24 px rather than shrinking the full mark.
- Place the lockup on paper, on ink, or on a flat brand colour.
- Use the mono mark, or the white lockup, when a single colour is required.

Incorrect:

- Do not stretch, squash or rotate any element.
- Do not recolour the mark, wordmark or tile outside the palette.
- Do not add drop shadows, glows or outlines.
- Do not re-set the wordmark in another typeface or with different tracking.
- Do not place the lockup on a busy photograph without a scrim.
- Do not put the mark inside a container that touches it; that is a clear-space violation.

## 4. Colour

One accent. Restrained neutrals. One categorical scale shared by every chart.

### Light - "paper"

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--paper` | `#f4f5f2` | Page background |
| `--surface` | `#fdfdfb` | Cards, panels |
| `--surface-2` | `#eef0ea` | Toolbars, chips |
| `--line` | `#e2e5df` | Hairlines |
| `--ink` | `#161a19` | Headings |
| `--ink-2` | `#38403d` | Body copy |
| `--muted` | `#5f6864` | Secondary text |
| `--faint` | `#68716c` | Tertiary text |
| `--accent` | `#2e6b57` | Primary action, active state |
| `--accent-ink` | `#235444` | Accent text on paper |
| `--accent-soft` | `#e2efe9` | Tints, focus halo |

### Dark - "ink"

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--paper` | `#0f1413` | Page background |
| `--surface` | `#171e1c` | Cards, panels |
| `--ink` | `#eef2ef` | Headings |
| `--ink-2` | `#c6cfc9` | Body copy |
| `--muted` | `#96a09a` | Secondary text |
| `--accent` | `#6fbfa0` | Accent |

Dark mode is not an inversion. The neutrals, the accent lightness and the map ramp are all re-tuned
so the hierarchy that works in light also works in ink.

### Data scales

Categorical, for mode share and similar charts: `--hue-1` to `--hue-5`.
Sequential, for walk access: `--band-1` (under 5 minutes) through `--band-4` (beyond 15 minutes).
Sequential, for station counts: `--primal-0` to `--primal-4`.

## 5. Typography

Two families, both open source, both self-hosted in the build. No font is fetched from a
third-party CDN at runtime.

| Role | Family | Weights | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display and interface | Instrument Sans | 400, 500, 600 | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| Data and metadata | JetBrains Mono | 400, 500 | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

Both licences permit commercial use, modification and embedding. Neither requires attribution in
the product, though crediting them is good practice. The wordmark is shipped as outlines, so the
logo itself carries no font dependency.

### Scale

Sizes are fluid where they need to be, fixed where they are read at one size.

| Role | Token | Range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | `--fs-display` | 40 to 76 px |
| Heading 1 | `--fs-h1` | 32 to 50 px |
| Heading 2 | `--fs-h2` | 25 to 34 px |
| Heading 3 | `--fs-h3` | 19 px |
| Body | `--fs-body` | 17 px |
| Small | `--fs-sm` | 15 px |
| Caption | `--fs-caption` | 13 px |
| Meta | `--fs-meta` | 12 px mono, uppercase |

Rules that go with the scale:

- Uppercase labels take `+0.14em` tracking. Never set caps without tracking.
- Display sizes take negative tracking (`-0.028em`). Never set large text loose.
- Body copy is capped at 68 characters per line.
- Numbers in tables, statistics and charts use tabular figures.

## 6. Motion

- Durations: `--dur-fast` 150 ms, `--dur-base` 280 ms, `--dur-slow` 560 ms.
- Easing: `--ease` for entry, `--ease-soft` for state changes.
- One orchestrated entrance per page, staggered by index. No scattered micro-interactions.
- Everything collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- Nothing animates that does not either reveal content, confirm an action, or show a state change.

## 7. Layout and shape

- Shape lock: surfaces 20 px, controls 10 px, chips fully rounded. One scale, everywhere.
- Structure is hairlines, not cards. A card is used only when elevation means something.
- Section rhythm: 72 to 120 px vertical.
- One accent use per screen region. Links are ink with a tinted underline, not accent-coloured.

## 8. Asset inventory

```
brand/
├── svg/      8 master files (mark, marks, monogram, wordmark, lockups)
├── png/      11 rasters including white and ink mono variants, 256 to 2400 px
├── favicon/  favicon-16/32/48, apple-touch-icon-180, android-192/512, site.webmanifest
└── social/   og-image.png and og-image-dark.png at 1200x630
```

Everything in `brand/` is regenerated by `npm run brand`, which reads the font from
`node_modules` and writes the SVG, PNG, favicon and social card set. The site renders the logo from
`src/lib/brand-paths.js`, which the same script generates, so the site and the downloads can never
drift apart.

## 9. Do not

- Do not use the mark at sizes below the minimums without switching to the monogram tile.
- Do not use emoji as interface icons.
- Do not introduce a second accent colour.
- Do not use the pre-restyle dark crimson palette; it has been retired with the old identity.
